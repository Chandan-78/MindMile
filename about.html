Who we are

Mind Mile Academy (also known as Mind Mile Institute) is an educational institute in Dillibazar, Kathmandu. Our main focus is helping students succeed in IOE, BE, B.Arch and CEE entrance exams.

We have guided 300 students so far and we are growing carefully, so every student still gets personal attention.

Our approach

  • Clear concepts before speed
  • Regular tests with feedback
  • Offline and online classes
  • Guidance for +2 and Cambridge students
